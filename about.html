Our Story

A School Built on Purpose & Faith.

Prescious Prime Stars Academy (PREPSA) was founded with a clear conviction: that every child is a star, gifted by God with unique potential waiting to be discovered, developed and deployed for greatness.

From our earliest days, we have been committed to providing a nurturing, disciplined and academically rigorous environment where children can flourish — not only in their studies, but in character, faith, leadership and creativity.

Today, PREPSA stands as a trusted community school in Abeokuta, serving families across Ogun State who desire a premium, values-driven education for their children — from Crèche through Junior Secondary School.
