Welcome to PREPSA

A Place Where Every Child Shines Bright.

Prescious Prime Stars Academy is a co-educational institution committed to nurturing well-rounded learners from Crèche through Junior Secondary. We blend academic rigour with Christian values, leadership training and creative innovation.

Our purpose-built campus in Apete, Obantoko, provides a safe, stimulating environment where curiosity is nurtured and every child is empowered to discover and develop their God-given potential.
